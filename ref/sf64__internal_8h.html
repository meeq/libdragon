Detailed Description

On-disk layout and runtime state of the SF64 sound bank format.

Author
Giovanni Bajo giova.nosp@m.nnib.nosp@m.ajo@g.nosp@m.mail.nosp@m..com

SF64 is the host-converted form of a SoundFont 2 bank: resolved presets and regions plus embedded WAV64 samples. See tools/audioconv64/conv_sf64.cpp.

struct sf64_envelope_t

DAHDSR envelope in SF2 units (timecents).

For the amp envelope, sustain_gain is linear amplitude (1.0 = peak). For the mod envelope, it is the unit sustain level (1.0 = peak) after the SF2 sustain-attenuation conversion done by audioconv64.
