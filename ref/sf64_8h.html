Detailed Description

SoundFont 2 banks converted to SF64.

Author
Giovanni Bajo giova.nosp@m.nnib.nosp@m.ajo@g.nosp@m.mail.nosp@m..com

SF64 is the runtime format produced by audioconv64 from a SoundFont 2 (.sf2) file. Load a bank with sf64_load and free it with sf64_close. Playback is performed by sf64_synth_t (see sf64_synth.h).
