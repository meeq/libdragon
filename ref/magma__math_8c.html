Preview API:
This API is part of the libdragon preview: it may change without notice. Set LIBDRAGON_PREVIEW=1 (warnings) or 2 (silent) in your Makefile to use it.

The resulting view space coordinate system is right handed, with positive X pointing right, positive Y pointing up, and positive Z pointing towards the camera (out of the screen).
